## Series

| Series | Pillar | Rhythm | Slot | Format | Status | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before and after | Build and hand over | Biweekly | Tuesday | Carousel | Approved | One anonymized client situation per issue: what it looked like, what was built, what runs now without me. Same structure as the website case studies |
| One number | Data before campaigns | Biweekly | Thursday | Single graphic | Approved | A single figure from real work with one line of context |
| Workflow of the week | AI as the method | Biweekly | Thursday | Text only | Proposed | One AI workflow, what it replaced, how long it took to set up, what the team does with the time. No cost figures |

Series alternate: Before and after on Tuesdays of even ISO weeks, One number and Workflow of the week alternate on Thursdays. Posts outside a series are free slots for any pillar.

## Format mix

Over eight weeks about one third carousels, one third single graphics, one third text only. A carousel needs a card with a before, a build and an after. A single graphic needs one number with a source line. Everything else is text.
