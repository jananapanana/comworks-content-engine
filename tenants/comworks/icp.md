# ICP: who the content is for

Source: intake block B, board database "2 ICP" (four personas, 22.09.2026). Economic buyer is C-level, the counterpart runs the system afterwards and decides whether it survives. Content has to make the counterpart look good to their boss.

## Company pattern

**Core (buyers):** mid-market B2B or B2B plus B2C, 50 to 500 employees, a sales team, a marketing function of one to five people, a CRM that exists but does not carry the business yet. A founder or C-level who has decided "we should be more systematic about sales". Revenue roughly 5 to 100 million euros `[CHECK]`.

**Added 2026-10-31 (Jana):** Director and VP level in enterprise and Series B companies. They do not buy the whole build, they own one piece of the revenue system (demand gen, sales ops, RevOps) and need arguments and methods they can carry into their own organisation. Series B here means funded, with revenue and a sales team; it is not the "early-stage without revenue" case below.

**Reading along (not a buyer persona):** solopreneurs and founders of small agencies. They recognise the situations in "Opinions on GTM" and "AI as the method" and share them. No series is written for them.

**Industries won so far:** logistics and shipping platforms, legal services, restaurant tech SaaS, wealth management and fintech, education, hospitality, retail, AI consultancy, deep tech ecosystems.

**Avoid:** pure white-label agency work, early-stage startups without revenue, companies that want a HubSpot admin by the hour, anyone who wants AI as the headline.

## Beliefs to shift (across personas)

- "We need more leads." Usually the existing ones need to be qualified and worked
- "A CRM project takes six months and an agency." It is a 60 to 90 day build if the ICP is decided first
- "If the consultant leaves, it stops." The build only counts if the team runs it afterwards
- "AI is a separate project." It is a layer on the system
