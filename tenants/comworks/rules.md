# Hard rules for COM works content

Checked by `core/checks.md` on every card and draft. Nothing here can be relaxed through `/ce-learn`.

## Never say

- Tool names as COM works' own label ("HubSpot consultant", "Clay expert"). Tool names are fine in hook and copy when they describe the reader's situation or the evidence (Jana, 06.10.2026)
- AI as the product. "AI does it for you", "AI-powered", "AI agency". AI is the method
- Cheaper, or any hourly-rate framing. No day rates, no prices, not even COM works' own
- Numbers the vault cannot back
- Anything that praises COM works. The work speaks, Jana reports

## Client money

No costs, prices, day rates, budgets, tool spend, contract values, invoice figures or revenue figures of any client. Ever. Outcome percentages and counts are allowed (open rate, filtered share, accounts enriched, hours saved).
