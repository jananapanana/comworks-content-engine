# Sample 2: Demographic scoring finds the middle, not the best

Single graphic, Thursday, LinkedIn personal. Board status In progress. Pillar: Data before campaigns. Series: One number.

**Hook:** We looked at the 50 highest-revenue accounts of a B2B platform. Industry, headcount, founding year, country: no pattern.
**Alt hook:** Four of five scoring dimensions measured the wrong thing. Here is how we found out.

Buying behaviour had one. Shipping zone, tariff type, carrier mix, order frequency separated the top 50 from the rest every time.

Four of the five dimensions in their lead score were demographic. The score measured the axis that doesn't separate anyone.

The fix is two clocks. An entry score on day 0 from what the customer tells you at sign-up. An account score from day 30 onwards from what they actually do.

If your score is all firmographics, it is a filter for the middle.
