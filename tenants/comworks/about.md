## One sentence

COM works builds B2B companies the system between interest and revenue: bring the data together, set up the sales automations, enable the team and AI, then hand over.

## Services

- **Strategy:** ICP, positioning, messaging, campaign strategy
- **System:** data orchestration, enrichment, scoring, automations, buying signals on the CRM as operating system
- **Campaigns:** the lifecycle and outbound campaigns that run on that system, including copy
- **AI and team enablement:** agents, skills, workflows plus team training, then handover and fade-out
- **Fractional GTM operator:** embedded, one to three days a week, capped at two or three clients

**We want more of:** build-and-transfer projects at a fixed price with a defined exit. Each one is the audition for an operator mandate. `[CHECK]` product ladder from 24.07. never confirmed

## Known for

- **Today:** the person who makes the CRM data usable. Referrals say "she sorts out our CRM"
- **In twelve months:** the person who builds the revenue system and leaves a team that runs it without her

## Alternatives the buyer considers

- HubSpot admins and freelancers by the hour: they configure, they do not decide what the system is for. COM works starts at the ICP and ends at the campaign
- GTM and RevOps agencies: they stay. COM works builds to leave
- In-house marketing manager: possible, usually stalls between tools. COM works has done the build several times and brings the templates
- AI consultancies: they sell AI. Here AI is the method
